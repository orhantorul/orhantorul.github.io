What changed persistence?

Change between the 1940–1950 and 1975–1985 birth cohorts, in percentage points.

StructuralCompositional

Structural change captures shifts in the probability of matching a parent within each parental education category. Compositional change captures shifts in the shares of parents across categories. The Shapley decomposition averages both orderings.

LowMediumHigh

Low includes lower secondary education or below, medium includes upper secondary and post-secondary non-tertiary education, and high includes tertiary education. Each respondent counts once.

Paper & data

Estimates use eleven rounds of the European Social Survey. The study covers native-born citizens aged 25 or older at interview and uses the better-educated parent as the measure of educational background.
