PhDMacroBook

PhDMacroBook is a rigorous textbook designed for first-year Ph.D. sequences and advanced master’s courses in macroeconomics. Edited by Marina Azzimonti, Per Krusell, Alisdair McKay, and Toshihiko Mukoyama, the volume bridges foundational modeling with contemporary research frontiers. The first ten chapters develop core theory, providing essential analytical frameworks and recursive methods. Subsequent chapters explore specialized research areas, offering state-of-the-art perspectives on current macroeconomic questions. For further details, visit the official website.
